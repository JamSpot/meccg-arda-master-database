### The key research question
The original Gandalf Gene spreadsheet and the published Council summary currently produce different checksums. This site deliberately records both until card-by-card evidence explains the difference.

> **No proxies. Genuine physical cards only.**

The end goal is an audited card inventory, quantities by expansion, provenance and an acquisition/shopping list.
