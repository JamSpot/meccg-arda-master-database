## Current verified quantity baseline

The explicit ARDA quantities in the original GandalfGeneArda.xls total **699 physical cards**: METW 552, METD 105, MEDM 41 and MEBA 1. Two named rows — Army of the Dead and The White Tree — have blank quantities and remain unknown. The published summary figure of 695 is not supported by those explicit quantities.

## Project principles

- Gandalf Gene's published Hero Arda list is the canonical comparison point.
- Alternative lists and rules variants remain separately identified.
- Quantities are recorded with their source; unknown values are never guessed.
- Only genuine physical MECCG cards are in scope — no proxies.
- Minion Arda is excluded unless specifically requested.
