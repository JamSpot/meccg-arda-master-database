## What Arda is

In the Arda Basic Game, all players draw from the same shared decks. It is designed for 2–7 players, encourages improvisation and trading, and uses a different preparation and play structure from conventional constructed Hero-vs-Hazard MECCG. The standard shared piles are the Play Deck, MP Deck, Roving Character Deck and Minor Item Offering Deck.

## Latest research update — 20 November 2026

Completed the expansion reconciliation for both 2024 **Arda Intro Game** pools and compared the all-rarities list with Gandalf Gene. The no-rares version contains 461 The Wizards + 93 The Dragons = 554 physical cards, including paired sites. The all-rarities version has 625 copies assigned to expansions (METW 514, METD 89, MEDM 15, MELE 6, MEAS 1) plus 69 cross-set-ambiguous hazard copies, for 694 physical cards. Against Gene, 298 identities are shared, 77 resolved identities are Intro Game-only, 76 are Gene-only, and 23 hazard identities remain expansion-ambiguous; 14 shared identities have different quantities. These are separate teaching variants, not replacements for Gandalf Gene. The research also clarifies that Basic Game site decks and Intro Game MP/site pairs must be stored differently.

## Current quantity baseline

The explicit quantities extracted from the original Gandalf Gene spreadsheet total **699 physical cards**:

| Expansion | Explicit copies |
|---|---:|
| The Wizards (METW) | 552 |
| The Dragons (METD) | 108 |
| Dark Minions (MEDM) | 32 |
| The Lidless Eye (MELE) | 6 |
| The Balrog (MEBA) | 1 |
| **Total** | **699** |

The source also names **Army of the Dead** and **The White Tree** with blank quantities; both are METW, but their quantities remain unknown. CCG Collector's 3 June 2020 community post reports 695 physical copies, four fewer than the 699 explicit rows. The Gene workbook's static Desglose tab totals 691, but it matches the separate Jose-san pool category table and is treated as stale, not as the Gene summary.

These are working extraction figures, not a claim that every card identity and expansion assignment has been independently verified. See the [card inventory](docs/card-inventory.html) and [reconciliation page](docs/source-reconciliation.html) for detail.

## Project principles

- Gandalf Gene's published Hero Arda list is the canonical comparison point.
- Multiplayer rules, solo adaptations, card lists and optional variants remain clearly distinguished.
- Every quantity should retain its source and reconciliation status.
- Only genuine physical MECCG cards are in scope — no proxies.
- No Dream Cards, fan-made/custom cards or unreleased material.
- Minion Arda is excluded unless specifically requested.
- Conventional Hero Solo Academy lessons and decklists do not belong in this Arda project.


### Additional source reviewed — Arda Rules 5.1 (12 November 2026)

The user supplied the original Council of Elrond **Arda Rules 5.1.pdf** attachment (version posted 28 May 2012). It is a primary rules reference, not a decklist. The forum editor says it made no major rules changes; the notable Expanded World addition is the Ring Special Item Deck, alongside suggested home rules and editorial refinements. The PDF gives flexible deck-size guidance and distinguishes the Basic Game's 20-MP Council from Expanded World's 25-MP threshold. Its Virtual Card Set 1, custom Arda card designs and altered-card text are excluded from the physical ICE-only inventory. No canonical quantities changed.
