### The key research question
The original Gandalf Gene spreadsheet extraction currently yields 374 distinct names and 699 physical copies among numeric-quantity rows. The workbook's own summary says 695, while the published expansion subtotals add to 376 rather than 377. These conflicts are recorded explicitly; no final shopping list should be treated as definitive until the row-level audit is complete.

> **No proxies. Genuine physical cards only.**

The goal is an audited card inventory, quantities by expansion, provenance, and an acquisition list.
