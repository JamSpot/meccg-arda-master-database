# Source watch — 25 October 2026

## Summary

This pass identified **specific downloadable catalogue files** behind the MeCCG community's lists-and-downloads page. They add useful independent references for card identity, expansion and rarity checks. They do **not** provide a newly discovered canonical Arda list or evidence that changes the known Arda copy totals.

## Newly documented direct downloads

1. **[MeCCG List of Cards — XLS spreadsheet](https://meccg.es/meta/cards/list/MeCCG%20List%20of%20Cards.xls)**  
   A downloadable Excel catalogue linked from the [MeCCG lists and downloads page](https://meccg.com/cards/lists-and-downloads/). The landing page does not show a version or publication date for this file. Treat it as a candidate cross-check for card names, expansions and rarity until its contents and coverage have been parsed; do not assume it supersedes the separately listed 2010 BGG catalogue.

2. **[MeCCG Full Spoiler List — PDF](https://meccg.es/meta/cards/MeCCG%20Full%20Spoiler%20List.pdf)**  
   A 198-page card reference organized by expansion and card type, including card text and rarity markers. Its own front matter says it was compiled by the Black Council of Mordor and updated in February 2007 to reflect errata. Useful as an independent identity/expansion check, but old and not an Arda quantity list.

3. **[Fixed Sets — PDF](https://meccg.es/meta/cards/list/Fixed%20Sets.pdf)**  
   A one-page reference linked from the same downloads page. It may help distinguish fixed-set product contents from Arda pool quantities; it should not be used to infer the Gene list's quantities.

## Published decklists: what they can and cannot establish

The [MeCCG deck database](https://meccg.com/decks/) exposes many published constructed decklists, some with explicit quantities, sideboards and card pools. In this search, no new downloadable Arda-specific spreadsheet or alternative canonical Arda pool was identified. Ordinary tournament/constructed lists are useful for strategy and card-combination research, but they must remain separate from the canonical Arda inventory.

The [BoardGameGeek MECCG files archive](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/1915/middle-earth/files) remains the relevant place to obtain the separately listed **MECCG Card List v1.13 (XLS, 6 March 2010)**, **Starter Set Decks Lists (XLS)** and **Arda deck example (PDF)**. The Arda example is not a spreadsheet.

## Canonical quantity status — unchanged

The extracted Gandalf Gene workbook remains the only source currently used for canonical pool quantities:

| Expansion | Explicit copies |
|---|---:|
| The Wizards (METW) | 552 |
| The Dragons (METD) | 105 |
| Dark Minions (MEDM) | 41 |
| The Balrog (MEBA) | 1 |
| **Total** | **699** |

The workbook's published summary still says 695. Army of the Dead and The White Tree have blank quantity cells. The four-copy discrepancy and the distinct-card-count discrepancy remain unresolved; no quantities have been changed on the basis of this search.
