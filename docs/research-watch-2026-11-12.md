# Research watch — 12 November 2026

## Summary

This pass reviewed the Council of Elrond Arda forum index and several older Arda discussions for independently published Hero card pools, spreadsheets and deck files. It found one downloadable Arda-labelled card archive that must be explicitly excluded: the 2010 **Arda Expanded World** printable cards are community-created/custom cards, not original ICE releases. A separate 2023 cooperative Arda proposal provides a useful pile-structure lead but no completed list or quantities.

**No new eligible ICE Hero Arda pool or quantity spreadsheet was verified. The canonical inventory is unchanged.**

## 1. Council of Elrond Arda forum index

The [Arda forum index](https://www.councilofelrond.org/forum/viewforum.php?f=37) currently lists 46 topics. The recent 2025–26 entries visible in the index include Dream Card / Minion Arda variants, which are outside this project's scope. No new original-card Hero Arda quantity list is exposed by the index.

The forum index remains valuable because it groups older published Arda material in one place, including Gandalf Gene, Thorondor's no-rares sample, Kober's solitaire rules, the Arda Intro Game and rules-variant discussions.

## 2. Arda Expanded World printable cards — explicitly excluded

The [2010 “Cards for Arda Expanded World” thread](https://www.councilofelrond.org/forum/viewtopic.php?f=37&t=1345) describes a separate set of revised Arda cards created with the Magic Set Editor tool. The author says the cards were made available as printable PDFs and MSE source files. The thread's downloadable [Arda cards Eric — characters ZIP](https://www.councilofelrond.org/forum/download/file.php?id=265) is listed as 1.77 MiB.

The post also identifies separate character, unique-resource, non-unique-resource and hazard files. These are custom Arda additions, not original ICE-published cards. They are recorded as an **excluded source** only; no card names or quantities are imported into the master database. The old MegaUpload PDF links in the post are no longer reliable.

A later reply in the same thread explicitly distinguishes Arda Expanded World material from Dream Card / Virtual Card sets. Both categories remain excluded here. The word “Arda” in a file title is not sufficient evidence that its contents are original ICE cards.

## 3. Other community variants checked — no new pool

### ARDA VARIANTS (2024)

The [ARDA VARIANTS discussion](https://www.councilofelrond.org/forum/viewtopic.php?f=37&t=4992) compares the Basic Game, Gandalf Gene's house rules, a “Wizards of ME” variant and a Winter/Lure 2024 variant. The latter uses a separate Tested Ring Deck, deals four Minor Items and lets each player keep two, and has a four-player maximum in the author's summary. These are rules comparisons, not separate card-quantity lists. The already-recorded [Arda Intro Game PDF](https://www.councilofelrond.org/forum/download/file.php?id=4315) and [Arda_eng.pdf](https://www.councilofelrond.org/forum/download/file.php?id=4286) are rules references, not new inventories.

### Arda Co-op? (2023)

The [Arda Co-op? proposal](https://www.councilofelrond.org/forum/viewtopic.php?f=37&t=4771) starts from Gandalf Gene's pool and proposes eight piles: Quest, Play, Ring, Minor Item, Character, Creature, Event and Sauron. It is an unfinished cooperative-format proposal, with questions still open about loss conditions and deck tuning. No complete card list, spreadsheet or expansion quantities are supplied. It is not a new independent pool.

### BanalityBob's Arda (2020)

The [forum post](https://www.councilofelrond.org/forum/viewtopic.php?f=37&t=4324) links a public [Google Sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1a_32IVLD7SkX6A8fqVsAz95i67HtcxAb4VZYuvxg8ns/edit?usp=sharing). The visible browser preview still exposes only the first seven populated rows and does not allow a complete export in this environment. The author describes separate resource and hazard decks, themed hazards, altered Ring handling and no Underdeep sites. Treat this as a distinct community variant and incomplete lead, not a complete countable card pool.

## 5. Canonical quantity baseline — unchanged

The original Gandalf Gene workbook remains at 699 explicit copies:

| Expansion | Explicit copies |
|---|---:|
| The Wizards (METW) | 552 |
| The Dragons (METD) | 108 |
| Dark Minions (MEDM) | 32 |
| The Lidless Eye (MELE) | 6 |
| The Balrog (MEBA) | 1 |
| **Total** | **699** |

CCG Collector's independent community report remains 695 copies, leaving four unresolved. *Army of the Dead* and *The White Tree* remain named rows with blank quantities. The separate distinct-card subtotal is 376 against the post's 377 headline; that is a card-identity counting issue, not the four-copy discrepancy.

No quantities have been added, removed or reallocated. The no-rares, Jose-san, Intro Game, solo and community-variant pools remain separate from the canonical Gene inventory.

## 6. Next research actions

1. Continue attempting to retrieve the original Gandalf Gene and Thorondor XLS binaries for row-level verification and hash comparison.
2. Recheck the BanalityBob sheet or locate a downloadable/exportable copy before treating it as a countable list.
3. Search for independently authored Hero Arda lists using only original ICE cards.
4. Keep custom Expanded World, Dream Card, Virtual Card, Minion Arda and proxy material excluded.

## Scope

Only genuine, originally published ICE MECCG cards are eligible. Dream Cards, fan-made/custom cards, unreleased material, proxies and Minion Arda quantities remain excluded.
