# Sources & Downloads

## Project scope

Only original, officially published ICE Middle-earth CCG cards are eligible for the master card database and quantity comparisons. Dream Cards, fan-made/custom cards, unreleased cards and proxies are excluded. Minion Arda deck recommendations are out of scope.

### Council of Elrond — Which sets are best for ARDA? (2015–2020 discussion)
https://councilofelrond.org/forum/viewtopic.php?t=2494

Useful context for interpreting the Gandalf Gene Hero pool. A January 2015 reply distinguishes Hero and Minion Arda as separate pools, recommends a Wizards base with selected Dragons/Dark Minions cards, and cautions against agents in Arda. In June 2020, CCG Collector reports the 377-card / 695-copy summary. Follow-up discussion suggests Dragon Ahunt manifestations and Drakes were omitted from the Gene build because they could be disproportionately punishing in Arda, while the hazard mix was tuned toward Orcs, Trolls and Men. These are community observations, not confirmed author notes or official rulings; they help distinguish deliberate pool curation from missing-card errors.

### BoardGameGeek — My Solo Arda Build with Session Report
https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1058232/my-solo-arda-build-with-session-report

Published 22 October 2013, with later turns added on 14 November 2013. Describes a personal solo-Arda adaptation using separate MP, Ring, Resource, Minor Item, two Character, Hazard and Site decks. The author generally uses one of each owned card and selected multiples of common resources/hazards, and excludes The Lidless Eye, The White Hand and The Balrog. The tested scenario targets 25 Marshaling Points in eight turns and draws hazards at four times the site's printed number when travelling, twice when staying. The author describes the rules as experimental and makes some ad hoc solo rulings. No exact quantity spreadsheet or deck file was verified. This is a solo-method comparison, not a canonical card-quantity source.

### MECCG Card List v1.13 — full catalogue copy
A 1.58 MB local workbook named `MECCG.xls` is available in the project Library. Its embedded metadata identifies **Version 1.13**, dated 6 March 2010, matching the BGG archive listing. It contains 1,722 named entries across 10 set codes, but only 1,674 have original ICE card IDs across the seven official card expansions. The remaining 48 entries have N/A IDs and come from three ICE sourcebooks: 16 Hands of the Healer scenario entries, 16 A Long-Expected Party site-map entries and 16 Northern Waste site entries. The Northern Waste sourcebook explicitly says its new location entries are not official MECCG cards as published; A Long-Expected Party provides site panels on a map, and Hands of the Healer includes scenario sites for casual play. They are not standard printed MECCG card records and are excluded from the physical-card master inventory. The seven eligible set counts are METW 492, METD 184, MEDM 183, MELE 419, MEAS 170, MEWH 122 and MEBA 104. It was used to cross-check 377 Gandalf Gene source rows and all 370 Jose-san ARDA rows. See the [v1.13 set-code audit CSV](../data/catalogue-v1-13-set-audit.csv) and [card-level catalogue audit](../data/catalogue-v1-13-audit.csv).

### Published Arda Rules 4.0 PDF

https://lureofmiddleearth.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/03/arda_4.0_en.pdf

A 25-page published Arda rules reference credited to Joe Bisz (concept) and Joe Bisz / Eric Dané (design). The Basic Arda Game is shared-deck multiplayer (2–7 players) and recommends a 250+ Play Deck with equal resources and hazards, about 15 MP cards per player (often around 100), a Roving Character Deck of about 70 for larger games, and a 30–35 card Minor Item Offering Deck. These are general sizing guidelines, not exact card quantities. The 1:1 Play Deck recommendation differs from the current Gandalf Gene transcription (171 resources and 252 creatures/hazards); preserve that difference rather than silently changing source counts.

## Source hierarchy

For reconstructing the canonical Gene deck:
1. Original Gandalf Gene deck/spreadsheet
2. Original Council post description
3. Derivative transcriptions/checksums (not independent unless provenance is separate)
4. Historical discussions and separately labelled Arda variants

6. Deckbuilding software as a technical cross-check only


### The Arda Intro Game — Jared Brayshaw (May 2024)

A 14-page community introductory Arda PDF, last edited 14 May 2024. [Download arda_intro_game.pdf (127.6 KiB)](https://www.councilofelrond.org/forum/download/file.php?id=4315). The Library copy is 130,663 bytes, matching the published attachment size.

The PDF includes two itemized sample pools. A 4 November 2026 row-level normalization is now published in [the card inventory CSV](../data/arda-intro-game-card-inventory.csv) and [expansion summary CSV](../data/arda-intro-game-expansion-summary.csv). The author's 505/606 totals omit the MP-paired site cards, giving 554/694 physical card components when those sites are included.
- **METW + METD only, no rares:** Play Deck 330 (141 resource events, 93 hazard events, 96 hazard creatures); MP Deck 49; Character Deck 57; Minor Item Deck 34; Ring Deck 10; Avatar Deck 5; Haven Deck 20. Total across all listed piles: **505**.
- **All rarities and sets:** Play Deck 360 (156 resource events, 107 hazard events, 97 hazard creatures); MP Deck 88; Character Deck 71; Minor Item Deck 41; Ring Deck 21; Avatar Deck 5; Haven Deck 20. Total across all listed piles: **606**.

This is an Arda Intro Game teaching variant based on the Compiled Arda Ruleset — Hero, Arda Revised and Gandalf Gene. It uses an MP deck with sites paired to resources, plus separate Character, Minor Item, Ring, Haven and Avatar piles. It is not a conventional constructed Hero-vs-Hazard solo list and is not the canonical Gandalf Gene pool.

Spot-checks against the Council of Elrond card database map entries such as Lindion the Oronín (DM-177, Hero), Emerald of Doriath (TD-112, Hero), Habergeon of Silver (TD-120, Hero), Valiant Sword (TD-161, Hero), Wormsbane (TD-172, Hero), Adamant Helmet (TD-96, Hero), Scabbard of Chalcedony (TD-150, Hero), The Arkenstone (TW-341, Hero), The Mithril-coat (TW-345, Hero), The One Ring (TW-347, Hero) and The Iron Crown (TW-496, Hero). The same database contains separate Minion-alignment printings for some similarly named cards; the Hero IDs must be used.

The PDF includes **Dreams of Lore**, which is the original ICE Hero card TW-210 from The Wizards, not a Dream Card. No custom Dream Cards are included in the project inventory.

The author’s section totals are recorded as published, but independent row-by-row reconciliation and expansion-by-expansion quantity totals remain outstanding. Do not merge these sample quantities into Gandalf Gene or Jose-san.

The current [MeCCG Lists and Downloads page](https://meccg.com/cards/lists-and-downloads/) links a [MeCCG List of Cards XLS](https://meccg.es/meta/cards/list/MeCCG%20List%20of%20Cards.xls), a one-page [Fixed Sets PDF](https://meccg.es/meta/cards/list/Fixed%20Sets.pdf), a [Challenge Decks ZIP](https://meccg.es/meta/cards/list/Challenge%20Decks.zip) and a three-page [Challenge Decks player aid PDF](https://meccg.es/meta/cards/list/MECCG-CD-playeraid_v1.0.pdf). The XLS and ZIP were identified by their direct links and MIME types but their binaries could not be extracted in this pass. The Fixed Sets PDF lists five 24-card Hero fixed packs and five 24-card Minion fixed packs; it is a starter-product reference, not an Arda pool. The player aid provides card-by-card lists and quantities for the ten preconstructed Challenge Decks, not an Arda list.

The [Samwise repository](https://github.com/SylvanSign/samwise) contains ten plaintext `.deck` files in its [decks folder](https://github.com/SylvanSign/samwise/tree/main/decks), covering Challenge Decks A–J. Its README describes standard-card support (no Dream Cards), local human-readable deck files and Cardnum-derived metadata. The file format separates Pool, Characters, Resources, Hazards, Sideboard and Sites, and includes expansion abbreviations. It is useful for testing deck-file parsing and set-code normalization, not for Arda quantities.

A comparison of all ten files with the MeCCG Challenge Deck player aid found that the A–E files list 19 sideboard cards, while the player aid specifies 20 including a backup Wizard. In the A deck, the player aid identifies Alatar as the backup Wizard, but this entry is absent from the Samwise sideboard. The A file's main counts (8 characters, 30 resources, 30 hazards and 15 sites) match the player aid. Prefer the player aid for complete Challenge Deck lists; treat Samwise files as convenient but potentially incomplete transcriptions. The Samwise latest release listed is v0.8.0-alpha (28 February 2022).

The Council of Elrond database remains a current card-text/errata reference, not a quantity source. Its README requires acknowledgement and notification before third-party projects use its data. No card data was copied from it into this project during this pass.

See the [8 November research watch](research-watch-2026-11-08.html) for direct downloads, the detailed comparison and the extraction limitations. None of these references changes the Gandalf Gene 699-copy working total or resolves the 695-versus-699 discrepancy.
