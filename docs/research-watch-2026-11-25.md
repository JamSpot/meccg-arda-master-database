# Research Watch — 25 November 2026

## Current community play confirmed, but no new ICE-only Hero pool

A Council of Elrond report posted on **15 September 2026** records actual Arda Hero play at the Lyon gathering held on 12–13 September:

- Four players played a Hero Arda game on Saturday evening, with a new player joining.
- The report explicitly says the group used Julien's Arda and that it **also contained Dream Cards**.
- A separate two-player Hero Arda game was played on Sunday, with Pierre winning 28–23. The report does not identify that game's exact pool.

Source: [Tournoi à Lyon les 12 et 13 Septembre 2026 — event report](https://www.councilofelrond.org/forum/viewtopic.php?f=77&p=43724).

This is useful evidence that Hero Arda remains actively played in 2026, including by a newcomer. It is **not** a qualifying card-pool source for this project: the only identified four-player pool explicitly contains Dream Cards, and the report provides no list, attachment, expansion totals or quantities. Julien's pool has not been reconstructed from the match narrative.

## Latest Arda-board activity checked

The Council of Elrond Arda forum index currently shows 46 topics. Its latest 2026 Arda topic is **The Nine are Abroad (The Nine DC version)**, posted 23 September 2026. The author describes a nine-player Dream Card Minion Arda box and says it is available through the Mellon deckbuilder.

Source: [Council of Elrond Arda forum](https://councilofelrond.org/forum/viewforum.php?f=37) · [The Nine are Abroad](https://councilofelrond.org/forum/viewtopic.php?t=5369).

This is explicitly Minion and Dream Card material, so it is excluded from the Hero / original-ICE master database. It does not alter any quantities.

The other recent full Arda list reviewed remains **The Houses of the North**, a Dwarves-themed Dream Card build. Its author reports 888 cards and 5 sleeve colours, but it includes Dream Cards and unreleased-set testing, so it is also excluded. See [the original discussion](https://councilofelrond.org/forum/viewtopic.php?t=4531).

## Downloadable files and quantities

No new downloadable original-ICE Hero Arda spreadsheet, CSV or deck file was verified in this pass. In particular:

- Julien's 2026 Lyon Arda list was not published in the event report.
- The 2026 Nine are Abroad list is available through a deckbuilder, but is Dream Card Minion Arda and outside scope.
- The 888-card Houses of the North list is posted as forum text; no separate XLS/XLSX or deck attachment was identified.

No card rows or expansion totals have been added. The canonical Gandalf Gene baseline remains **699 explicit copies**: METW 552, METD 108, MEDM 32, MELE 6 and MEBA 1. The 699-versus-695 discrepancy, plus the blank Army of the Dead and The White Tree quantities, remain unresolved.
