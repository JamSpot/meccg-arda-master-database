# Research watch — 7 November 2026

## Summary

This pass added source-level detail for the canonical Gandalf Gene Arda build and clarified how it differs from other community Arda rulesets. It did **not** find a new independently verified card-quantity pool, so the master quantities remain unchanged.

## 1. Original Gandalf Gene post — exact published structure

[Council of Elrond: Gandalf Gene's Arda Deck](https://councilofelrond.org/forum/viewtopic.php?f=37&t=2870) was posted by Goblin King on 26 December 2016. The post identifies the format as a 2–5 player community build and links the 79 KiB `GandalfGeneArda.xls` spreadsheet (attachment ID 945).

The post gives the Play Deck composition explicitly:

| Play Deck component | Published quantity |
|---|---:|
| Resource cards | 171 |
| Creature and hazard cards | 252 |
| **Play Deck total** | **423** |

It also documents the other piles and important boundaries:

- **Resources:** a One Ring strategy is included, but the post says the only way to win with the One Ring is to use Gollum and *Gollum's Fate*.
- **Hazards:** mainly The Wizards; focused on Orcs, Trolls and Nazgûl, with Dragons, a few Men and a Spider. All *Dragons* and *Dragons at Home* are included; *Dragons Ahunt* are excluded.
- **MP Deck:** most Marshaling Point resources come from The Wizards, with hoard items from The Dragons and a card or two from Dark Minions.
- **Roving Character Deck:** all characters are used, including promotional characters and Strider from The Balrog.
- **Minor Item Offering Deck:** three copies of most minor items, with special draft priority for certain unique items.
- **Sites:** no Underdeep sites.

The setup sequence in the post is also explicit: each player selects a Wizard; receives two major characters; drafts a starting company from a pool of ten characters; selects two starting minor items, with priority rules for Black Arrow, Emerald of Doriath, Sting and Thorin's Map; draws eight Play Deck cards and five MP cards; may trade MP cards one-for-one; and starts at Riverside or the Wizard's home site.

**Ruleset distinction:** this post says 2–5 players, whereas the published Arda Rules 4.0 Basic Game supports 2–7. It is therefore a particular community Arda build, not a replacement for the general Basic Game rules. Its 171:252 Play Deck ratio also differs from the 1:1 resource/hazard construction guidance in Arda Rules 4.0. Preserve both source statements rather than “correcting” the Gene list to fit the generic recommendation.

The original post says it uses no optional Arda card errata. Its own setup and drafting instructions remain part of this particular build and should be taught as such, not silently generalized to every Arda table.

**Download:** [GandalfGeneArda.xls — Council of Elrond attachment ID 945](https://www.councilofelrond.org/forum/download/file.php?id=945). The forum page lists it as 79 KiB. The binary could not be re-fetched through the research browser in this pass; the already-extracted project copy remains the working source pending a binary hash comparison.

## 2. CardGameGeek mirror — useful but derivative

[CardGameGeek: Gandalf Gene's Arda Deck](https://cardgamegeek.com/database/decks/27774d75-5a7d-4509-ba89-8cb556f3bfc5) explicitly says it was recreated from the Council of Elrond post. Its record is labelled `Main (178)` and its author notes that two hazards are missing.

This is a derivative transcription, not an independent Arda pool. It can help spot-check card names and individual quantities, but it must not replace the original workbook, establish a new total, or be merged into the master database. The original Council post and spreadsheet take precedence.

## 3. Arda Revised and the 2019 community comparison

BoardGameGeek lists [Arda Revised](https://boardgamegeek.com/filepage/98645/arda-revised), by SpiralDawn, dated 4 January 2014, as a 3 MB all-in-one rules PDF. It is a rules variant, not a card-quantity spreadsheet.

The Council of Elrond discussion [Arda Ten Years Later](https://www.councilofelrond.org/forum/viewtopic.php?p=32480) explains why these variants must remain separate:

- Arda Revised changes how the MP pile is presented and drawn (two MP cards rather than five), revises ring handling and permits a sage to tap to use a Palantír.
- The Gandalf Gene build is described as a more focused resource/hazard pool, with its own character and minor-item drafting changes.
- Other groups report modified Gene pools with later-expansion cards or small numbers of roadblock hazards, and a separate optional face-up major-quest-card variant.

These are useful historical comparisons, but none supplies a new complete, verified physical card-quantity list for the canonical database. Any separate virtual/custom-card experiment mentioned in the discussion is outside this project's scope.

## 4. Quantity status — unchanged

The current Gandalf Gene extraction remains:

| Expansion | Explicit physical copies |
|---|---:|
| The Wizards (METW) | 552 |
| The Dragons (METD) | 108 |
| Dark Minions (MEDM) | 32 |
| The Lidless Eye (MELE) | 6 |
| The Balrog (MEBA) | 1 |

Two METW rows, *Army of the Dead* and *The White Tree*, still have blank quantities. CCG Collector's independent 2020 report remains 695 copies, four fewer than the explicit workbook rows. No source found in this pass resolves those four copies or changes the expansion totals.

## Project boundary

Only genuine, officially published ICE cards are eligible. No Dream Cards, fan-made/custom cards, unreleased material, proxies or Minion Arda quantities are included. Promotional printings remain identified separately from base expansion card counts.
