# Source watch — 28 October 2026

## Summary

This pass found a previously untracked **published solo-Arda build with a session report**: BoardGameGeek's *My Solo Arda Build with Session Report*, published 22 October 2013 and edited with additional turns on 14 November 2013.

It provides useful evidence about how an Arda-style card pool can be adapted for solo play. It is **not** a new canonical card-quantity source and does not resolve the 699-versus-695 discrepancy.

## New comparison source

- **Source:** [BoardGameGeek — My Solo Arda Build with Session Report](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1058232/my-solo-arda-build-with-session-report)
- **Format:** forum post and session report. No downloadable spreadsheet or exact deck file was verified.
- **Scope:** the author excludes The Lidless Eye, The White Hand and The Balrog.
- **Structure:** separate Marshaling Point (MP), Ring, Resource, Minor Item, two Character, Hazard and Site decks.
- **Character split:** one deck for characters with Mind 6 or higher and another for Mind 5 or lower.
- **Quantity philosophy:** generally one of each owned card, with extra copies of broadly useful resources and common hazards. The author says the exact mix is personal; no complete exact card-by-card quantity list is supplied.
- **Solo approach:** the player also acts as the Hazard player, drawing from a large hazard pool and choosing combinations to attack the Hero company. The indexed session report mentions a Dragon Ahunt combining with a dragon-attack modifier.
- **Project use:** a practical comparison for the separate **Arda Solo** route. It is not the same as normal constructed-deck solo play, and not the same list as Gandalf Gene's 423-card Arda Play Deck.

Direct automated opening of the BGG thread is blocked (HTTP 403), so this summary uses indexed post text; comments and possible attachments could not be independently inspected.
