# Source watch — 28 October 2026

## Summary

This pass found a previously untracked **published solo-Arda build with a session report**: BoardGameGeek's *My Solo Arda Build with Session Report*, published 22 October 2013 and edited with additional turns on 14 November 2013.

It provides useful evidence about how an Arda-style card pool can be adapted for solo play. It is **not** a new canonical card-quantity source and does not resolve the 699-versus-695 discrepancy.

## New comparison source

- **Source:** [BoardGameGeek — My Solo Arda Build with Session Report](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1058232/my-solo-arda-build-with-session-report)
- **Format:** forum post and session report. No downloadable spreadsheet or exact deck file was verified.
- **Scope:** the author excludes The Lidless Eye, The White Hand and The Balrog.
- **Structure:** separate Marshaling Point (MP), Ring, Resource, Minor Item, two Character, Hazard and Site decks.
- **Character split:** one deck for characters with Mind 6 or higher and another for Mind 5 or lower.
- **Quantity philosophy:** generally one of each owned card, with extra copies of broadly useful resources and common hazards. The author says the exact mix is personal; no complete exact card-by-card quantity list is supplied.
- **Solo approach:** the player also acts as the Hazard player, drawing from a large hazard pool and choosing combinations to attack the Hero company. The report includes a Dragon Ahunt combining with a dragon-attack modifier.
- **Tested setup and win condition:** randomly draw a Wizard and place it at Rivendell; draw a Mind 6+ character, then lower-Mind characters until the 20 direct-influence limit is reached or exceeded by the next draw. Start with two random minor items, five Resource cards and three MP cards. Keep four face-up minor items available to find and a face-up line of recruitable characters.
- **Experimental solo rules:** play for eight turns and aim for 25 Marshaling Points; draw hazards equal to four times the site's printed number when the company travels, or twice that number when it stays. The solo player chooses the most harmful legal hazards from the drawn hand, up to the hazard limit. The author labels some rules as playtesting and explicitly describes making ad hoc decisions when a card behaves awkwardly in solo play, so this is a prototype variant rather than a polished official ruleset.
- **Project use:** a practical comparison for the separate **Arda Solo** route. It is not the same as normal constructed-deck solo play, and not the same list as Gandalf Gene's 423-card Arda Play Deck.

Direct automated opening of the BGG thread is blocked (HTTP 403), so this summary uses indexed post text; comments and possible attachments could not be independently inspected.

## Additional catalogue check: archived MECCG card-list spreadsheet

I inspected the locally available MECCG_Card_List.xlsx copy against the working Gandalf Gene inventory. It contains **144 catalogue rows**, only **45 normalized card-name overlaps** with the 374-card numeric inventory, and its rarity column is blank throughout. Some matching names carry a different set label, consistent with a catalogue that records particular printings rather than proving the source printing used in the Arda workbook.

**Conclusion:** this file is a partial metadata cross-check only. It is not complete enough to verify the full pool, and its set labels must not silently overwrite the canonical workbook's expansion assignments. It contributes no quantity evidence and does not explain the four-copy difference.
