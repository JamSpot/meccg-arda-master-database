# Source watch — 26 October 2026

## Summary

This pass surfaced a substantial, itemized **888-card Dream Cards Arda list**, “The Houses of the North,” on the Council of Elrond forum. It is a useful additional published Arda design to compare structurally, but it is **not a replacement for Gandalf Gene's standard Hero Arda pool**: it is Dwarf-themed, includes Dream Cards and some unreleased-set experiments, uses optional Dream Cards rules/UEPs, and separates cards into several piles including a sideboard.

No newly found source changes the canonical quantity baseline or resolves its four-copy discrepancy.

## Additional published Arda list: The Houses of the North

- **Source:** [ARDA with Dream cards — The Houses of the North](https://councilofelrond.org/forum/viewtopic.php?t=4531)
- **List size stated by author:** 888 cards.
- **History:** originally posted 3 November 2021; the forum post records later edits, most recently 30 July 2025.
- **Format:** itemized forum text, not a downloadable XLS/CSV attachment. The author groups cards by play function/pile and includes quantities and set abbreviations.
- **Distinctive design:** Dwarf-focused; five Wizard avatars; separate marshalling-point, character, minor-item, ring and sideboard piles; optional Dream Cards rules and UEPs; some cards from unreleased sets are used for testing.
- **Use for this project:** keep as a **separate variant comparison**. It can inform how other Arda designers group piles and document quantities, and its standard-card entries may be useful for later card-identity cross-checks. Do not merge its counts into the canonical inventory: the pool, card legality and rules assumptions differ, and custom Dream Card set codes cannot be mapped to official expansions without a card-by-card check.
