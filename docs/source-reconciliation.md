# Source Reconciliation

## The central discrepancy

Published Council summary:
- 377 cards
- 695 physical cards

The extracted workbook has:
- 377 named rows
- 375 rows with numeric quantities
- 374 distinct names among numeric rows
- 376 unique named identities overall after deduplicating Nenseldë the Wingild and retaining the two blank-quantity rows
- 699 explicit physical copies

## Published expansion figures

| Expansion | Published distinct cards |
|---|---:|
| METW | 300 |
| METD | 59 |
| MEDM | 14 |
| MELE | 2 |
| MEBA | 1 |
| **Expansion subtotal sum** | **376** |

The subtotals match the 376 unique named identities once expansion assignments are cross-checked. The headline 377 likely counts the duplicate Nenseldë row, although the source author's counting method is unconfirmed.

## Verified working inventory

| Expansion | Distinct cards with numeric quantities | Explicit copies |
|---|---:|---:|
| METW | 298 | 552 |
| METD | 59 | 108 |
| MEDM | 14 | 32 |
| MELE | 2 | 6 |
| MEBA | 1 | 1 |
| **Working total** | **374** | **699** |

The two additional blank-quantity identities, Army of the Dead and The White Tree, have blank quantities. The verified working inventory corrects Peril Returned to METD and Uruk-Lieutenant/Wild Trolls to MELE, based on the independent [MECCG card catalogue](https://elvenforest.narod.ru/meccg/cards/meccg_list.html). These are metadata corrections only; all source quantities are retained.

## Still unresolved

The published physical-copy total is 695, but the numeric source rows sum to 699. The four-copy difference must be traced to the source rows or the Desglose calculation. It is not explained by the three expansion corrections, the duplicate Nenseldë identity, or the two blank quantity cells.

## Reconciliation plan

1. Recalculate the workbook's Desglose totals by category from raw rows.
2. Compare every quantity-bearing row against the original workbook and independent transcription.
3. Verify remaining expansion and rarity fields against the Council of Elrond card database and catalogue.
4. Locate the four-copy difference.
5. Produce an audited acquisition table with provenance for every quantity.

Until the copy-count audit is complete, the inventory is improved but still not a final shopping list.
