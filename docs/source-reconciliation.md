# Source Reconciliation

## The central discrepancy

Published Council summary:
- 377 distinct cards
- 695 physical cards

Direct spreadsheet extraction:
- 374 distinct cards among numeric-quantity rows
- 699 physical copies

The spreadsheet also contains 377 named rows if the two blank-quantity rows are retained.

## Published expansion figures

| Expansion | Distinct cards |
|---|---:|
| METW | 300 |
| METD | 59 |
| MEDM | 14 |
| MELE | 2 |
| MEBA | 1 |
| Published total | 377 |

The listed figures themselves add to 376, creating a second source/arithmetic discrepancy.

## Spreadsheet working figures

| Expansion | Distinct | Copies |
|---|---:|---:|
| METW | 298 | 552 |
| METD | 58 | 105 |
| MEDM | 17 | 41 |
| MEBA | 1 | 1 |
| Working total | 374 | 699 |

These are not interchangeable totals.

## Reconciliation plan
1. Match every spreadsheet row to a canonical card identity.
2. Check duplicate names and alternate printings.
3. Compare set and rarity against MECCG Card List v1.13 and the Council card database.
4. Identify whether the two blank-quantity rows are intended inclusions.
5. Identify the four-copy difference between published and spreadsheet totals.
6. Explain the expansion-count discrepancy without silently rewriting the source.
7. Produce an audited acquisition table with provenance for every quantity.

Until complete, the spreadsheet totals are labelled unreconciled.
