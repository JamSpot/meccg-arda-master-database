# Source Watch — 21 November 2026

## Executive result

The Council of Elrond forum has yielded a useful comparison of several community Arda rule variants, plus a 2025 discussion that documents one way players manage the Minor Item Offering. These sources improve the variant and deck-construction record, but **they do not provide a new complete card-quantity list**.

No new original-ICE-only Hero Arda pool or downloadable card-quantity spreadsheet was verified in this pass. No card quantities have been changed.

## 1. Newly reviewed: Council of Elrond — ARDA VARIANTS

[ARDA VARIANTS discussion](https://councilofelrond.org/forum/viewtopic.php?f=37&t=4992), opened 1 March 2024 and updated through 23 October 2024, compares several distinct table practices. It is a community comparison, not an official rules document. Its author notes that the Winter/Lure rules were reconstructed from conversations and may need correction.

| Variant described in the thread | Shared piles | Character / Wizard setup | Minor Item selection | Player limit / Council |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Arda Basic Game, version 5 | Play, MP, Minor Offering, Roving Character | Select Wizard; two characters of 6 mind or more plus ten other characters in the draft pool | Choose one at a time | Up to 7; 20 MP |
| Gandalf Gene build | Same four piles | Select Wizard; two characters of 7 mind or more plus Gimli; special Strider/Aragorn and Fram Framson rules | Choose two at a time; special priority for selected unique items | Up to 5; 25 MP |
| Goblin King's Wizards of ME variant | Same four piles | Gene-like major-character draft; Strider/Aragorn removed from the opening draft and recruited later | Choose one at a time | Up to 5; 20 MP |
| Winter/Lure 2024 practice | Five piles, adding a Tested Ring Deck | Random Wizard; no Roving Characters available on turn one | Deal four at random; keep up to two | Up to 4; reported Council thresholds 18, 20 or 25 MP |

The comparison also records special rules for certain character-specific cards and treasure. These details explain why a deck list cannot be detached from its setup rules and treated as a universal Arda pool.

**Important:** the 25 MP target in the Gene build is not the 20 MP Basic Game threshold. The Winter/Lure entry is a reported local practice, not a standard rule. Do not combine these setups or thresholds without choosing a variant first.

The same discussion contains CCG Collector's March 2024 Intro Game description. It explains that each MP card is paired with a site, the site is flipped to its resource side when used rather than physically tapped, and a company may not visit a site paired with a resource held by one of its characters. It also uses a separate Tested Ring Deck. The downloadable [Arda Intro Game PDF](https://www.councilofelrond.org/forum/download/file.php?id=4315) remains a distinct teaching pool, not a replacement for Gandalf Gene.

## 2. Newly reviewed: How manage special minor items?

The [July 2025 discussion](https://councilofelrond.org/forum/viewtopic.php?f=37&t=5230) records one community member's practice:

- Reveal four Minor Items face up at the start.
- A player claims an item by going to a site where that item is playable.
- At the start of each turn, discard the left-most offering and reveal a replacement at the right.

The thread also contains a different player's house-rule approach, which ignores Hoard restrictions and special items. Goblin King explicitly cautions that Arda is not an official ICE format and says the four-face-up, site-appropriate offering is how he has played it.

This is useful evidence of a published community practice, **not** proof that every Arda group must use this exact procedure. It changes no card quantities. See the [deck-construction guide](building-arda-decks.html) for the practical storage implications.

## 3. BanalityBob's Arda spreadsheet rechecked

The [2020 forum post](https://councilofelrond.org/forum/viewtopic.php?f=37&t=4324) links to a [Google Sheets list](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1a_32IVLD7SkX6A8fqVsAz95i67HtcxAb4VZYuvxg8ns/edit?usp=sharing) and a separate rules document.

The browser-visible sheet currently exposes only seven populated rows: Gandalf, Saruman, Radagast, Alatar, Pallando, Fram Frampson and Saw Further and Deeper. The remaining visible rows are blank. It is therefore not a complete card-pool or quantity source in the accessible version.

The forum author describes a personal variant with non-standard character and Minor Item choices, separate Resource and Hazard decks, themed Hazard decks for each player, different Ring handling and no Under-deeps because the author did not own all the sites. Keep it as a variant lead; do not import its visible rows into the canonical inventory.
