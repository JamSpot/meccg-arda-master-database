# Research watch — 23 November 2026

## Executive result

No new complete original-ICE Hero Arda decklist, card-pool spreadsheet or downloadable deck file was verified in this pass. The canonical quantity baseline is unchanged.

The meaningful addition is a closer source review of two 2019 Council of Elrond discussions. Together they clarify that "Arda" has historically included materially different card-text and pool approaches. This matters when building a definitive database: printed ICE identity, current official errata, and optional Arda/Virtual/custom text must not be collapsed into one record.

## 1. Arda Ten Years Later — an important card-text distinction

[Arda Ten Years Later](https://www.councilofelrond.org/forum/viewtopic.php?t=3944), opened and reviewed in full, contains several useful statements:

- CCG Collector describes the earlier Arda rules as including Elf-Lords, Arda-specific altered card text associated with Virtual Card Set 1, and scenario-specific Arda cubes such as Dragon Hunt and Northern Wastes.
- Goblin King distinguishes the Gandalf Gene build from that earlier approach: Gene's list was intended to use no non-errata card text and to create more focused resource and hazard strategies.
- Goblin King also describes other Hero Arda variants that modify the Gene pool by adding later-expansion cards and some roadblock hazards.

**Database implication:** keep three kinds of information distinct:
1. Original ICE printed card identity, set and printing.
2. Current Council of Elrond errata / official rules text.
3. Optional Arda-specific, Virtual Card, Dream Card or custom text and cards.

Only the first category determines whether a physical card belongs in the original-ICE master inventory. The second is a rules-text reference. The third must remain in a separate optional-variant record and is excluded from this project's card quantities.

The discussion is historical community evidence, not a new quantity list.

## 2. ARDA Play through — a four-player session report, not a decklist

[ARDA Play through](https://www.councilofelrond.org/forum/viewtopic.php?f=37&t=3813) records a four-player game from 14–16 April 2019. It is useful as a practical example of how a shared pool is drafted and used:

- The author chooses a Wizard after considering the two major characters, opening resources and treasure options.
- The report shows a company built around Círdan, Thorin II and Boromir, with cards such as An Unexpected Party, The Dwarves Are Upon You, Ringlore and Palantír of Orthanc appearing during play.
- The author describes using small, sturdy two-character parties to reduce the number of hazards opponents can play against a company.

This is an illustrative session report only. It does not give a complete card inventory, expansion totals or quantities. The named cards are examples of cards seen in one game, not evidence of how many copies belong in the pool.
