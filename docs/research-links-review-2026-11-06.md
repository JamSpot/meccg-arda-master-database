# Research links review — 6 November 2026

This page records the investigation of the user-supplied MECCG research bookmarks. The links are classified by what they actually provide. A link being useful for ordinary MECCG play does not make it an Arda card-pool source.

## Findings that matter directly to Arda

### Kober — Arda Solitaire v0.2 (Council of Elrond, 4 April 2015)

- [Forum topic and downloadable PDF (44.23 KiB)](https://www.councilofelrond.org/forum/viewtopic.php?f=37&t=2578)
- [Direct PDF attachment](https://www.councilofelrond.org/forum/download/file.php?id=819)

The forum post explicitly presents this as a way to play Arda when friends are unavailable. The attachment is a rules document, not a decklist or card-quantity spreadsheet. The forum page records 2,328 downloads when checked.

The follow-up discussion adds useful distinctions:
- In May 2016, a commenter contrasted Kober's method with Callasmar's BGG variant: Callasmar pairs a site with a playable card; Kober's method retains travel to the relevant site for Greater/Major Items, Information and Gold Rings.
- Kober replied that themed Arda-solitaire builds are possible and desirable, mentioning his own Trolls-burning-Rohan-and-Gondor theme and an Orcs-of-Angmar theme.

**Research status:** the forum discussion is verified, but the PDF attachment could not be retrieved for full extraction in this pass. Therefore no setup, turn sequence, hazard count, scoring rule or card quantity has been inferred from the PDF. Keep Kober separate from Callasmar and the 2013 BGG solo-Arda session report. The forum discussion includes a separate participant's comments about invented cards; those are not Kober's rules and do not enter this project. Only official ICE cards remain eligible.

### Spanish MECCG forum — deck index and strategy archive

- [Mazos y estrategias forum](https://meccg.es/foro/viewforum.php?f=16)
- [Índice de mazos](https://meccg.es/foro/viewtopic.php?t=1759)

The forum section currently reports 248 topics across nine pages. Its index, originally posted in 2007 and last updated in 2021, groups many conventional Hero, Minion and Balrog constructed decks by archetype and theme. The checked index did not identify an Arda shared-pool list. Treat these as conventional constructed-deck examples, not Arda quantity sources.

Two more recent threads were inspected:
- [Juego en Solitario](https://meccg.es/foro/viewtopic.php?t=6899) (December 2025, follow-up July 2026): a player seeks help creating multiple themed constructed decks from their collection, each with its own hazards. No decklist or card quantities are supplied.
- [Estrategias de Juego Alternativo](https://meccg.es/foro/viewtopic.php?t=5147): casual scenario and house-rule ideas, including fixed-turn games and a Dragon Lord variant. It is not an Arda pool.
- [El juego "un mazo sencillo"](https://meccg.es/foro/viewtopic.php?t=6016): a request for a straightforward, casual constructed deck; no finished list is present.

### MeCCG.com published deck archive

- [Deck archive](https://meccg.com/decks/)

The current archive has searchable constructed deck records and filters for alignment, opponent alignment and competitive results. Its published examples include tournament decks and archetypes. The checked archive did not expose an Arda shared-pool list. It is useful for ordinary constructed deck strategy and card-combination research, but its decklists must not be merged into Arda quantities.

### Cardnum — online card browser, deckbuilder and play platform

- [Cardnum cards/deckbuilder](https://cardnum.net/cards)
- [Council of Elrond Cardnum forum](https://www.councilofelrond.org/forum/viewforum.php?f=146)

Cardnum is a browser-based MECCG card browser, deckbuilder and play environment. Its card interface includes filters such as set, type, alignment, rarity, released-only and hide-Dream-Cards. For this project, any use must be constrained to released original ICE cards; Dream Cards, extras and unreleased entries are excluded.

Cardnum is a useful tool for checking card availability and building/testing conventional decks. It is not an independent physical card catalogue or an Arda quantity source.

### Cardnum Deck Build Helper — downloadable Excel workbook

- [Council of Elrond topic: Offsite Deck Build Helper](https://www.councilofelrond.org/forum/viewtopic.php?f=146&t=4912)
- [Download Cardnum_deckholder_v04.xlsx (1.63 MiB)](https://www.councilofelrond.org/forum/download/file.php?id=4505)

The author describes this as a self-contained Excel deck-building helper, updated in June 2025. It includes card lookup data, rarity/type/alignment fields, card counts, deck-building sheets, World-winning decks, Progressive-game decks and 20 decks taken from official MECCG books. It also includes Dream Card rarity lookup data and Cardnum JSON data (the author reports 4,382 records, including possible blank/template entries).

**Relevance:** a potentially useful secondary deck-list and metadata workbook, not an Arda shared-pool spreadsheet. It must be filtered/audited before any data is reused. Dream Card rows and any unreleased/custom entries are excluded. The forum post says some tournament deck entries have missing starting items or other cards and advises checking the linked original source before relying on a list.

### Council of Elrond — Cardnum offline deck helper discussion

The Cardnum forum also documents an earlier Excel helper for preparing deck lists offline and pasting them into the web deckbuilder. This is a workflow aid, not a published Arda pool. See the [Cardnum forum index](https://www.councilofelrond.org/forum/viewforum.php?f=146).

## Useful but non-Arda deckbuilding and card-reference sources

### Trevor Stone — Middle-earth deck index and archetypes

- [Middle-earth Deck Index](https://trevorstone.org/metw/decks/)
- [Hero resource deck archetypes](https://trevorstone.org/metw/decks/heres.html)
- [Hazard deck archetypes](https://trevorstone.org/metw/decks/hazards.html)
- [MECLU card lookup](https://trevorstone.org/metw/cards/)
- [Spoiler lists](https://trevorstone.org/metw/spoilers.html)

The deck index contains Hero resource and hazard archetypes, a dragonslaying/general deck, an all-commons auto-attack hazard portion and a casual combo deck. The resource archetype article predates The Lidless Eye and is explicitly Hero-only. MECLU is a searchable card reference for The Wizards and The Dragons. These are useful for conventional Hero deck construction and older card-text checks, not Arda quantity sources or a complete seven-set modern catalogue.

### Mark's Deck Building article

- [Deck Building](http://mark.random-article.com/meccg/deck-building.html)

A novice-oriented Hero constructed-deck guide. It recommends choosing a compact travel area, selecting matching sites and MP cards, and balancing resources with the intended site network. It is strategy guidance, not an Arda rules or quantity source.

### Solo Scenarios for Middle-Earth CCG — Getting Started

- [Getting Started](https://meccg.wordpress.com/getting-started/)

This article distinguishes Arda's solo variant from the official solo rules and says Arda reduces the need for conventional deckbuilding. It recommends Challenge Decks or The Wizards/The Dragons as entry points for ordinary Hero play. This is useful context, but it does not define the Arda card pool.

### Solo Scenarios for Middle-Earth CCG — My Solo Rules

- [My Solo Rules](https://meccg.wordpress.com/my-solo-rules/)

This is a separate, personal constructed-solo method, not Arda. It uses Resource, Hazard and Location decks; generally 30–40 resource cards and a similarly sized hazard deck; a rough 50/50 creature/general-hazard split; and about a dozen sites. The author says complete decklists are deliberately not supplied. Keep this method in the conventional solo research lane, not in Arda setup instructions.

## What changes in the Arda master database?

1. **No Arda deck quantities change.** The new links did not yield another verified Arda quantity list.
2. **Kober v0.2 is now a stronger documented Arda-solo lead.** Its forum discussion establishes the site-travel distinction from Callasmar, but the PDF still needs extraction before its full procedure can be taught.
3. **Cardnum_deckholder_v04.xlsx is a new downloadable research asset.** It contains many non-Arda deck examples and metadata, so it is a candidate for carefully filtered secondary comparisons, not an Arda pool.
4. **The Spanish and MeCCG.com deck archives are classified as constructed-deck references.** Their conventional decklists remain outside the shared Arda quantity tables.
5. **The personal solo articles remain separate from Arda.** They must not be used to fill gaps in Arda solo rules.

## Unchanged physical-card scope

Only original, officially published ICE cards are eligible. No Dream Cards, custom/fan-made cards, unreleased material or proxies are included. No Minion Arda deck recommendations are added. Promotional printings remain distinguished from base expansion quantities.
