# Research Watch — 24 November 2026

## New published community list: ChanSterling's 2018 Hero Play Deck

A previously unregistered Council of Elrond post, **Arda Deck Comments?**, dated 16 September 2018, links to a Google Sheet titled *Chan's Middle-Earth Arda*. The author says they had played two two-player games and wanted feedback on the list. They also say they were considering adding Arda-specific cards and printing virtual-card slips later. The linked sheet currently exposes a **158-copy Hero Play Deck list** with **79 named entries**.

Sources: [original forum post](https://www.councilofelrond.org/forum/viewtopic.php?f=37&t=3499) · [Chan's Google Sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1gMxILv8-HiQDyvG2eplMkiHSsCZwXt2yYZFN5Ssv3g4/edit)

### What the sheet contains

All 79 visible names map to Hero-aligned Resource cards in the Council of Elrond card database. The sheet exposes only this Hero resource component: it does not provide a hazard list, MP Deck, character supply, Minor Item Offering Deck, site list, or a complete shared-deck inventory. It is therefore a useful **partial community build**, not a complete Arda pool and not a replacement for Gandalf Gene.

Expansion assignments were normalized using the current Council of Elrond card database. In particular, **Wizard Uncloaked is The Dragons (TD-169 / METD)**, not The Wizards. The three Dark Minions expansion cards in this sheet are Hero-aligned resources; their expansion does not make them Minion-alignment cards.

### Comparison with Gandalf Gene

- **48 identities overlap**, representing 105 copies in Chan's list versus 140 copies in the Gene inventory.
- **31 identities / 53 copies are not present in Gene's explicit-quantity inventory**: METW 9 identities / 17 copies; METD 19 / 29; MEDM 3 / 7.
- Among the 48 shared identities, **25 quantities differ**: Chan uses fewer copies of 24 cards and more of one card.
- The clear increase is **Gates of Morning: five copies in Chan's list versus three in Gene**. This is a source-specific build choice, not a quantity to transfer into Gene.

## Deck-construction and physical-play findings

### Copy-count guidance

Arda Rules 4.0 describes a flexible construction approach and suggests roughly two or three copies of a non-unique card and one copy of a unique card. It also says deck sizes are not fixed. A 2020 community discussion debates adding extra duplicates to improve the chance of drawing or influencing away selected cards; the counterargument is that extra copies enlarge the shared deck and dilute other cards. Treat this as a variant/build decision, not as a reason to silently change a published list.

The 2018 Chan list contains five Gates of Morning, illustrating that individual community builds can depart from the general copy-count guidance.

Sources: [Arda Rules 4.0, deck construction](https://lureofmiddleearth.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/03/arda_4.0_en.pdf) · [Unique Cards in an Arda Deck?](https://www.councilofelrond.org/forum/viewtopic.php?f=37&t=4276)

### Handling a very large Play Deck

A June 2020 discussion recommends randomly splitting a large Arda draw deck into four or five stacks around the table. Any stack can be drawn from because they remain parts of **one shared deck**. This is a physical-access solution, not four or five separate card pools.

Source: [Stacking the Cards of Larger Decks](https://www.councilofelrond.org/forum/viewtopic.php?f=37&t=4226)

These are printable setup aids, not card lists.

### Excluded custom-card material

The [Cards for Arda Expanded World thread](https://www.councilofelrond.org/forum/viewtopic.php?f=37&t=1345) offers custom/altered Arda card material, including a surviving [characters ZIP attachment](https://www.councilofelrond.org/forum/download/file.php?id=265). The original post explicitly describes revised/custom Arda Expanded World cards and printable new text. These are excluded from the original-ICE card database.

The [Tabletop Simulator Arda discussion](https://www.councilofelrond.org/forum/viewtopic.php?f=37&t=4315) is useful for play-area ideas, but it does not expose a public named deck file: the described TTS setup uses unnamed card images and the customized savefile was offered by private message.
