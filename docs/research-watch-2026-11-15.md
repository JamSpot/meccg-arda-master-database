# Research watch — 15 November 2026

## Result

**No new published ICE-only Hero Arda card pool, card-list spreadsheet, or standalone Arda deck file was verified in this pass.** The canonical Gandalf Gene quantities remain unchanged. One new supporting deck-checking utility was identified and recorded as a secondary metadata aid, not as a card-pool source.

## 1. New supporting utility — Theo's MECCG deck tools

[Theo-Donly MECCG tools on GitHub](https://github.com/Theo-Donly/theo-donly.github.io/tree/main/MECCG/tools)

The repository contains three useful browser-based HTML utilities:

- [MECCG Deck Checker](https://github.com/Theo-Donly/theo-donly.github.io/blob/main/MECCG/tools/MECCG-Deck-Checker.html) — last modified 23 May 2026.
- [MECCG Deck Distinction](https://github.com/Theo-Donly/theo-donly.github.io/blob/main/MECCG/tools/MECCG-Deck-Distinction.html) — last modified 2 June 2023.
- [MECCG Collection Generator](https://github.com/Theo-Donly/theo-donly.github.io/blob/main/MECCG/tools/MECCG-Collection-Generator.html) — a collection-oriented companion utility.

The Deck Checker embeds 1,736 catalogue keys with card-name variants, card image identifiers, rarity, alignment, card type and related deck-checking metadata. This is **not** a count of distinct original ICE cards: the catalogue includes multiple alignment/site representations and naming conversions. It is a third-party utility, not an Arda list or a source of physical-copy quantities.

A targeted spot-check agrees with the existing v1.13 expansion audit for five cards: Army of the Dead (TW), The White Tree (TW), Peril Returned (TD), Uruk-lieutenant (LE) and Wild Trolls (LE). This is a secondary consistency check only; it does not replace the original catalogue or change any quantity.

## 2. Recheck of published archives and deck databases

- The [BoardGameGeek MECCG files archive](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/1915/middle-earth/files) currently shows 69 files. Its Arda-related entries include the previously catalogued Jose-san **Arda deck example XLS**, Arda Revised rules PDF and Compiled Arda Ruleset — Hero PDF. The two PDFs are rules references, not card pools. No newer eligible ICE-only Hero Arda inventory was surfaced.
- The same archive lists *The Road Goes Ever On* as a 64-card companion set for a separate solo ruleset. It is not presented as an Arda pool and is not imported.
- A targeted [CardGameGeek deck database](https://cardgamegeek.com/database/decks/27774d75-5a7d-4509-ba89-8cb556f3bfc5) search surfaced the existing Gandalf Gene transcription only. It describes itself as recreated from the Council of Elrond source and says two hazards are missing; it remains a derivative partial cross-check, not an independent list.
- The [MeCCG.com deck archive](https://meccg.com/decks/) continues to surface conventional constructed decks. No new shared-deck Arda inventory was identified there.
- Targeted Council of Elrond searches did not reveal a new eligible ICE-only Hero list or a new downloadable Arda XLS/XLSX/deck file.
