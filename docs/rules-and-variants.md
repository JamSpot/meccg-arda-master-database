# Rules & Variants

## Compiled Arda Ruleset — Hero
The March 2024 Compiled Arda Ruleset – Hero is a rules reference based partly on the Middle-earth Arda Game, Arda Revised and Gandalf Gene's Arda Deck.

It is not treated as a replacement cardlist for the 2016 Gandalf Gene deck.

Important structural features include specialised decks, separate sleeve colours, copy limits and later rule changes. The ruleset also records cards that should not be included under that rules version.

## Arda Revised
Arda Revised is a rules/deck-construction variant rather than a fixed Gandalf Gene cardlist. It uses a communal Play Deck and separately constructed character, MP, Ring and Minor Item decks.

## Intro / solo variant
arda_intro_game.pdf is an introductory streamlined variant adapted from several Arda sources, including Gandalf Gene's deck. Its published sample counts and deck structure are useful for learning and solo play, but are not evidence for the original Gene list.

## Spanish / simplified rules
TierraMediaF2.pdf contains a simplified Arda rules presentation with a solo mode.

## Principle
Rules variants can explain how a card is used, but should not silently alter the historical card inventory.
