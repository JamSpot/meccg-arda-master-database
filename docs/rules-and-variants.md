# Rules & Variants

## Start with the Arda Basic Game

The main Arda format is a shared-deck multiplayer game for 2–7 players. All players draw from common Play, Marshalling Points, Roving Character and Minor Item Offering piles. Use the [Arda Multiplayer Guide](arda-multiplayer-play.html) for setup and the complete Basic Game turn flow, based on the published Arda Rules 4.0.

## Published Arda Rules 4.0

The 2016 Rules 4.0 document describes the Basic Game, recommended pile sizes, multiplayer setup, player turns, trading, calling the Council and simultaneous active turns for 5–7 players. It also includes optional Expanded World and scenario material. Optional material should be agreed before play; do not silently mix it into the Basic Game.

[Read Arda Rules 4.0 (PDF)](https://lureofmiddleearth.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/03/arda_4.0_en.pdf)

## Gandalf Gene's 2016 community build — keep its setup separate

The [original Gene post](https://councilofelrond.org/forum/viewtopic.php?f=37&t=2870) describes a 2–5 player build, whereas Arda Rules 4.0 allows 2–7. Its Play Deck is 171 resources plus 252 creatures/hazards, rather than the generic 1:1 resource/hazard guidance in Rules 4.0.

The setup sequence and starting location also differ:

| Step | Arda Rules 4.0 Basic Game | Gandalf Gene 2016 build |
|---|---|---|
| Opening order | Draw 8 Play + 5 MP cards first | Select Wizard first |
| Characters | Draft Wizard, then deal/draft up to 10 characters | Deal two major characters, then draft from a pool of 10 |
| Minor items | Draft two after the hands and characters | Select two after the character draft, using priority rules for certain unique items |
| MP exchange | General trading rules apply during play | Explicit one-for-one MP hand swap before starting |
| Starting site | Rivendell or the Wizard's home site | Riverside or the Wizard's home site |

The Gene post also specifies a One Ring win condition requiring Gollum and *Gollum's Fate*, no Underdeep sites, and a curated hazard mix that includes all Dragons and Dragons at Home but no Dragons Ahunt. Treat the 2016 post's pile contents and setup as one coherent community variant. Do not combine individual steps with the Basic Game sequence without an explicit table decision.

## 2019 community retrospective — keep card-text versions separate

The Council of Elrond's [Arda Ten Years Later](https://www.councilofelrond.org/forum/viewtopic.php?t=3944) discussion explains an important historical distinction. CCG Collector describes earlier Arda material that used Elf-Lords, Arda-specific altered text associated with Virtual Card Set 1 and scenario cubes. Goblin King says the Gandalf Gene build instead uses no non-errata card text, while other Hero variants may add later-expansion cards and roadblock hazards.

For card-pool research, a card name alone is not enough to identify which version a list intended. Keep the original ICE printing, current official errata text and any optional Arda/Virtual/custom text as separate provenance layers. Only original ICE physical printings count toward this project's master card quantities; do not silently substitute Virtual or custom text.

The [April 2019 four-player ARDA Play through](https://www.councilofelrond.org/forum/viewtopic.php?f=37&t=3813) is useful as a practical example of drafting and play, but it is not a decklist and supplies no pool quantities.

## Compiled Arda Ruleset — Hero

The March 2024 Compiled Arda Ruleset – Hero is a rules reference based partly on the Middle-earth Arda Game, Arda Revised and Gandalf Gene's Arda Deck.

It is not treated as a replacement cardlist for the 2016 Gandalf Gene deck. It includes additional clarifications and optional variants.

[Find the compiled ruleset on BoardGameGeek](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/1915/middle-earth/files)

## Arda Revised

Arda Revised is a rules/deck-construction variant rather than a fixed Gandalf Gene cardlist. It uses a communal Play Deck and separately constructed character, MP, Ring and Minor Item decks.

[Find Arda Revised on BoardGameGeek](https://boardgamegeek.com/filepage/98645/arda-revised)

## Community variant comparison — do not mix these setups

The Council of Elrond's [ARDA VARIANTS discussion](https://councilofelrond.org/forum/viewtopic.php?f=37&t=4992) compares several community practices. It is a useful comparison, not an official rules document.

| Variant described | Shared piles | Opening / character practice | Minor Item selection | Players / Council |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Basic Game, version 5 | Play, MP, Minor Offering, Roving Character | Select Wizard; two characters of 6 mind or more plus ten other characters in the draft pool | Choose one at a time | Up to 7; 20 MP |
| Gandalf Gene build | Same four piles | Select Wizard; two characters of 7 mind or more plus Gimli; special Strider/Aragorn and Fram Framson rules | Choose two at a time; selected unique items have priority rules | Up to 5; 25 MP |
| Wizards of ME community variant | Same four piles | Gene-like major-character draft; Strider/Aragorn are removed from the opening draft and recruited later | Choose one at a time | Up to 5; 20 MP |
| Winter/Lure 2024 practice | Five piles, adding a Tested Ring Deck | Random Wizard; no Roving Characters available on turn one | Deal four at random; keep up to two | Up to 4; reported Council thresholds 18, 20 or 25 MP |

The comparison also records special handling for certain character-specific cards and treasure. Its author says the Winter/Lure details may need correction. Treat each column as its own variant; in particular, do not substitute the Gene build's 25 MP target for the Basic Game's 20 MP threshold.

The same thread includes CCG Collector's 2024 Intro Game description. That teaching variant pairs MP resources with sites, uses a separate Tested Ring Deck and changes how paired sites are handled. Its two card pools are separately inventoried in the [2024 Intro Game source watch](research-watch-2026-11-21.html); neither replaces Gandalf Gene.

A separate [July 2025 Minor Item discussion](https://councilofelrond.org/forum/viewtopic.php?f=37&t=5230) documents one community practice of revealing four Minor Items, allowing a player to claim one by visiting an appropriate site, and replacing the left-most offering each turn. The post explicitly says Arda is not an official ICE format. Treat this as a local practice, not a universal Basic Game rule.

## Arda-specific solitaire variants

Several solo adaptations have been identified. They are separate methods, not one combined ruleset:

- **2013 BGG solo build:** experimental personal variant with MP, Ring, Resource, Minor Item, two Character, Hazard and Site decks; the session report describes a 25 MP / eight-turn goal and hazard counts scaled differently for travelling and staying.
- **Kober Arda Solitaire v0.2 (2015):** separate solo rules PDF; the available comparison notes that it retains travel to appropriate sites for Greater/Major Items, Information and Gold Rings.
- **Callasmar solo-Arda variant:** described as pairing a site with a playable card, a different approach to site access.
- **Spanish simplified Arda (2022):** a separate Arda summary with a solitaire mode added; full procedure still requires extraction.

See the [Arda-specific Solo Play guide](arda-solo-play.html) for the source-by-source comparison and the limits of what has been verified. Do not import the conventional Hero Solo Academy's Hero/Hazard decks or expedition sequence into Arda.

## Arda Intro Game — multiplayer teaching variant

[Download the 2024 Arda Intro Game PDF (127.6 KiB)](https://www.councilofelrond.org/forum/download/file.php?id=4315). This is a separate 2–5 player introductory multiplayer variant adapted from several Arda sources, including the Compiled Arda Ruleset — Hero, Arda Revised and Gandalf Gene. It uses paired MP-resource/site cards and separate Character, Minor Item, Ring, Haven and Avatar piles. Its 505-card METW/METD no-rares pool and 606-card all-rarities pool are alternative teaching lists, not the canonical Gene pool. **It is not a solo rules source**; use the Arda-specific solo guide and the separately documented solo variants for solo play. The all-rarities list includes official promotional printings, which must be kept outside base expansion totals. See the [3 November research watch](research-watch-2026-11-03.html).

## Principle

Rules variants can explain how a card is used, but should not silently alter the historical card inventory. Keep multiplayer rules, solo adaptations, card pools and each optional rules version tied to their own source.
