# Rules & Variants

## Arda format rules
Arda uses a specialised format and pile structure that differs from ordinary constructed Hero-vs-Hazard solo play. Always identify the exact Arda rules document/version before building piles or applying optional rules.

## Compiled Arda Ruleset — Hero
The March 2024 Compiled Arda Ruleset – Hero is a rules reference based partly on the Middle-earth Arda Game, Arda Revised and Gandalf Gene's Arda Deck.

It is not treated as a replacement cardlist for the 2016 Gandalf Gene deck.

Important structural features include specialised decks, separate sleeve colours, copy limits and later rule changes. The ruleset also records cards that should not be included under that rules version.

## Arda Revised
Arda Revised is a rules/deck-construction variant rather than a fixed Gandalf Gene cardlist. It uses a communal Play Deck and separately constructed character, MP, Ring and Minor Item decks.

## Arda-specific solitaire variants
Several solo adaptations have been identified. They are separate methods, not one combined ruleset:

- **2013 BGG solo build:** experimental personal variant with MP, Ring, Resource, Minor Item, two Character, Hazard and Site decks; the session report describes a 25 MP / eight-turn goal and hazard counts scaled differently for travelling and staying.
- **Kober Arda Solitaire v0.2 (2015):** separate solo rules PDF; the available comparison notes that it retains travel to appropriate sites for Greater/Major Items, Information and Gold Rings.
- **Callasmar solo-Arda variant:** described as pairing a site with a playable card, a different approach to site access.
- **Spanish simplified Arda (2022):** a separate Arda summary with a solitaire mode added; full procedure still requires extraction.

See the [Arda-specific solo guide](arda-solo-play.html) for the source-by-source comparison and the limits of what has been verified. Do not import the conventional Hero Solo Academy's Hero/Hazard decks or expedition sequence into Arda.

## Intro / solo variant
arda_intro_game.pdf is an introductory streamlined variant adapted from several Arda sources, including Gandalf Gene's deck. Its published sample counts and deck structure are useful for learning and solo play, but are not evidence for the original Gene list.

## Principle
Rules variants can explain how a card is used, but should not silently alter the historical card inventory. Keep every solo method tied to its own source and version.
