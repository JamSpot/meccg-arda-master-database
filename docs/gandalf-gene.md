## Other pile structure and setup documented by the post

The author describes this as a 2–5 player community build. The post gives these details:

- **MP Deck:** most Marshaling Point resources are from The Wizards, with hoard items from The Dragons and a card or two from Dark Minions.
- **Roving Character Deck:** all characters are used, including promotional characters and Strider from The Balrog.
- **Minor Item Offering Deck:** three copies of most minor items, with special draft priority for selected unique items.
- **Sites:** no Underdeep sites.
- **One Ring:** the strategy is included, but the post says the only way to win with the One Ring is with Gollum and *Gollum's Fate*.
- **Hazard curation:** mostly Orcs, Trolls and Nazgûl, with Dragons, a few Men and a Spider; all Dragons and Dragons at Home are included, while Dragons Ahunt are excluded.

The post's setup is: select a Wizard; deal two major characters to each player; draft a starting company from a pool of ten characters; select two starting minor items, using priority rules for Black Arrow, Emerald of Doriath, Sting and Thorin's Map; draw eight Play Deck cards and five MP cards; allow one-for-one MP trades; then start at Riverside or the Wizard's home site.

The 2–5 player limit is specific to this build; it does not replace the 2–7 player range in the published Arda Rules 4.0 Basic Game. Likewise, the 171-resource / 252-hazard Play Deck differs from the generic 1:1 construction guidance in Arda Rules 4.0. Keep those source differences visible.

The original post says it uses no optional Arda card errata. Its own setup and drafting rules remain part of this particular build and should not be generalized to every Arda ruleset.

## Spreadsheet extraction
The supplied `GandalfGeneArda.xls` has sheets MECCG Cards and Desglose.

The MECCG Cards sheet contains 379 rows including title/header structure. Excluding the title row leaves 377 named card rows.

The quantity field behaves as:
- 1 = singleton
- 3 = triple copy
- blank = no quantity recorded

Two named rows have blank quantities:
- Army of the Dead
- The White Tree

Among rows with numeric quantities:
- 374 distinct card names
- 699 physical copies
- Nenseldë the Wingild occurs twice as separate rows, both quantity 1

Two quantity-3 rows also have missing rarity:
- Uruk-Lieutenant
- Wild Trolls

These findings require reconciliation with CCG Collector's 3 June 2020 community summary of 377 distinct cards / 695 physical copies. The workbook's static Desglose tab totals 691, but its categories match the separate Jose-san pool and include Minion categories absent from the Gene rows; it is stale and is not treated as the Gene quantity summary.
