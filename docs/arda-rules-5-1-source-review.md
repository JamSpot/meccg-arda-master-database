## Provenance and version history

The Council of Elrond thread says version 5.1 was added on 28 May 2012. Eric states that it made “no major rules changes,” added a few suggested home rules from other threads to Expanded World, and included visible teasers plus general editing/upgrading. He identifies the Ring Special Item Deck as the most noteworthy addition. The same post says earlier versions 4.0 and 5.0 were removed from the thread.

This makes the uploaded PDF a primary rules source for Arda version 5.1, not a new card-pool list.

## Basic Game — deck construction guidance

The PDF defines four shared piles:
- Play Deck: hazards and non-Marshaling-Point resources.
- Marshaling Points Deck: resources that yield MPs.
- Roving Character Deck: almost all available characters.
- Minor Item Offering Deck: a selection of minor items.

It also describes a Site Deck, ideally brought by each player, or shared as a reference with a record of visited sites.

The suggested sizes are guidelines, not fixed list quantities:
- Play Deck: about 50 cards per player; a standard deck 250+ cards, with equal resources and hazards.
- MP Deck: at least 15 cards per player, usually around 100.
- Roving Character Deck: generally all available characters (the text gives 70 Hero characters as an example); reserve two copies of each Wizard for 6–7 players.
- Minor Item Offering Deck: usually 35–40 cards, with two copies of most minor items.

The document explicitly says Arda deck sizes are not fixed and depend on collection size and expected player count. These recommendations must not be treated as a published card-by-card inventory or expansion quantity target.

## Basic Game — setup and play details worth retaining

- 2–7 players; shared-deck format.
- Each player draws a separate Play Hand of 8 and MP Hand of 5.
- Players draft Wizards, receive random characters (including two of mind 6 or higher), choose a starting company under normal 20-mind / five-character limits, then draft two minor items.
- Four roving characters and four minor items are revealed for shared availability.
- Start at Rivendell or the Wizard's home site.
- For more than four players, the document recommends simultaneous active turns.
- During hazard passing, the hazard limit is reduced by one only if the base hazard limit was greater than two at the start of the movement/hazard phase. If the base limit was two or less, passing does not reduce it.
- In the Basic Game, a player may call the Council at 20 MPs; all other players receive another turn.

These points are useful when checking or teaching the 5.1 Basic Game. They should not be silently substituted into other Arda variants.

## Expanded World — separate optional rules

The Expanded World section adds a Ring Special Item Deck; Gold Rings remain in the MP Deck. It sets the Council threshold at 25 MPs, makes Ring items and Hoard items separate MP categories, and includes further optional rules.

The forum's 2012 post specifically calls the Ring Special Item Deck the most noteworthy 5.1 addition. It also clarifies the distinction retained in the PDF: 20 MPs for the Basic Game and 25 MPs for Expanded World.

## Strict physical-card scope: exclusions

The PDF is not a pure original-ICE card list. Its Expanded World section includes:
- cards from Virtual Card Set 1;
- newly designed Arda characters, items, allies, factions, hazards and resource events;
- proposed text replacements and pen-and-ink card edits;
- suggestions to make some normally Minion cards legal in Hero play.

These are optional Expanded World material and do not qualify for this project's physical ICE-only master inventory unless an individual card is independently confirmed as an original ICE-published card in its original form. Do not import the custom card designs, Virtual cards, altered text, or cross-alignment house-rule additions into the canonical card quantities. This also does not authorize Minion Arda material.

## Comparison with Arda Rules 4.0 and the canonical card-pool work

The Council forum's version-history note is the best direct comparison available here: 5.1 was described by its editor as having no major rules changes, but it adds/surfaces some Expanded World home rules and editorial refinements. The Ring Special Item Deck is the standout addition. The 5.1 Basic Game remains a flexible shared-deck construction framework, not a fixed list.

The Gandalf Gene Hero Arda workbook remains the canonical quantity source. This PDF does not add, subtract or reassign any card copies. It cannot resolve the 699 explicit-copy total versus the 695-copy CCG Collector report, nor the blank quantities for Army of the Dead and The White Tree.
