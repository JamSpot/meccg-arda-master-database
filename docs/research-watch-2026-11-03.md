# Research watch — 3 November 2026

> **Correction — 4 November 2026:** The Hero Arda *The Arkenstone* entry is resolved to TW-341 (The Wizards, Hero, rare). The promo LE-418 is a separate Minion-alignment printing and is not used in this Hero pool. The full Arda Intro Game row-level and expansion audit is now in the [4 November research watch](research-watch-2026-11-04.html).

## Summary

A previously overlooked community document contains **two complete sample Arda Intro Game card pools**, including card quantities by deck section:

- **METW + METD, no rares:** 505 cards across all listed piles.
- **All rarities and sets:** 606 cards across all listed piles.

This is a meaningful additional published card-pool source. It is a separate 2024 introductory Arda variant, not a replacement for the canonical Gandalf Gene list. **No canonical Arda quantities have been changed.**

The source copy is a 14-page PDF titled *The Arda Intro Game*, by Jared Brayshaw, last updated May 2024. A public download URL was not identified in this pass; the PDF is present in the project research Library.

## 1. What the PDF contains

The introductory variant is designed for 2–5 players and is based on the Compiled Arda Ruleset — Hero, Arda Revised, and Gandalf Gene's Arda Deck. Its shared card structure differs from the basic multiplayer Arda arrangement:

- Play Deck
- Marshalling Points (MP) Deck, with each MP resource paired with a site
- Character Deck
- Minor Item Deck
- Ring Deck
- Haven Deck
- Avatar Deck

The site is paired with the relevant MP resource in a clear sleeve rather than being managed in a separate site deck. This is therefore an Arda-specific teaching variant, not a conventional constructed Hero-vs-Hazard solo deck.

## 4. Promotional-printing and checklist scope discovery

The current [Card Hunters HQ The Wizards checklist](https://cardhuntershq.com/browse/meccg/TW/checklist) reports **500 entries**. Its own source page identifies the Council of Elrond MECCG restoration archive as its data source, so it is a useful searchable mirror but **not an independent catalogue count**.

The checklist marks numerous rows as promotional (rarity P), including alternate promo printings of cards already present in the main list (for example Barrow-wight, Pick-pocket, Boromir II, Dark Quarrels and Escape), plus entries TW-492 to TW-500. Those later rows include Fury of the Iron Crown, Neeker-breekers, Black Arrow, Fatty Bolger, The Iron Crown, A Pack at the Door, Swordmaster, Dwarven Axe and Lure of MECCG.

The [MeCCG collecting archive](https://meccg.com/collecting/) separates English, German and other promo/misprint material. It lists The Iron Crown, The Arkenstone, Black Arrow, Fatty Bolger and Fury of the Iron Crown among English promos; A Pack at the Door, Swordmaster and Dwarven Axe appear in its German promo list. It places the Lure of MECCG special card under “Others”, not among the English ICE expansion cards.

### Consequence for the Arda Intro Game sample

The 606-card “all rarities and sets” sample contains at least four clearly identifiable English promo cards (their TW IDs are promo catalogue entries, not base-expansion copies):

- Black Arrow
- Fatty Bolger
- Fury of the Iron Crown
- The Iron Crown

The Arkenstone is also listed in the promo archive, but the Arda PDF names the card without identifying which physical printing is intended. That row needs a printing-level check.

These promo copies must be recorded in a separate **official promotional cards** category, not silently added to the seven base expansion totals. The source's 606-card total remains the author's whole-pool total; an expansion-only subtotal cannot yet be stated safely until the printing variants are resolved.

### Exact base-set versus promo reconciliation

The 484-versus-492 discrepancy is now resolved across all seven expansions. The metw.net PDFs count the base expansion cards; the v1.13 workbook has the same base rows plus 17 rows marked Promotion. Removing those 17 promo rows from v1.13 produces exactly the metw.net count in every expansion:

| Expansion | metw.net base cards | v1.13 rows | Promo rows | Base rows after promo exclusion |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| METW | 484 | 492 | 8 | 484 |
| METD | 180 | 184 | 4 | 180 |
| MEDM | 180 | 183 | 3 | 180 |
| MELE | 417 | 419 | 2 | 417 |
| MEBA | 104 | 104 | 0 | 104 |
| MEAS | 170 | 170 | 0 | 170 |
| MEWH | 122 | 122 | 0 | 122 |
| **Total** | **1,657** | **1,674** | **17** | **1,657** |

This is the definitive reconciliation for the base expansion catalogue: **1,657 base cards plus 17 promotional rows = 1,674 v1.13 rows**. The detailed table is stored in [catalogue-expansion-reconciliation.csv](../data/catalogue-expansion-reconciliation.csv).

Do not join card records across the v1.13 workbook and the Council of Elrond JSON database by TW number alone. The numbering schemes differ: for example, The Iron Crown is TW488 in the v1.13 workbook but TW-496 in the Council of Elrond database; v1.13 TW485–TW492 are its eight METW promo rows, whereas the CoE database uses overlapping TW-485–TW-491 IDs for region entries. Match by normalized name, set, type, alignment and printing status instead.

The Card Hunters HQ 500-entry checklist is a mirror of the CoE database, not an independent base-set count. It contains additional promo/special entries. Its TW-500 Lure of MECCG entry is classified by the MeCCG collecting archive under “Others” as a special card, not an original ICE expansion card, so it is excluded from the canonical inventory.

Lure of MECCG is present in the 500-entry mirror but is classified by the MeCCG collecting archive as a special card under “Others”. It is not an original ICE expansion card and is excluded from the canonical physical ICE expansion inventory. This is separate from Dream Cards; Dream Cards remain excluded as well.

## 6. Jose-san workbook: exact Hero-compatible expansion quantities

A row-level extraction was completed from the Library copy of ARDA.xls (311,808 bytes). It has 1,402 card rows across the seven expansions, with SET, TYPE, ALIGNMENT, RARITY, NAME, ARDA and FALTAN columns; site and region rows are not included, so it is an Arda-pool worksheet rather than the full catalogue. Its file properties name Jim Montanus as creator and José San Miguel as last editor (2 May 2010), matching the Jose-san Arda example listed in the BGG file archive. A binary hash comparison with the BGG attachment has not been completed, so the provenance match is strong but not hash-proven.

Only Avatar, Hero and Neutral rows were retained. Minion and Stage rows were excluded from this Hero research. The extracted pool has **322 Hero-compatible identities and 623 copies**. The important refinement is that 3 of those copies are official promotional cards, not base-expansion copies.

| Expansion | Base identities | Base copies | Promo identities | Promo copies | Total identities | Total copies |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| METW | 226 | 407 | 2 | 3 | 228 | 410 |
| METD | 61 | 126 | 0 | 0 | 61 | 126 |
| MEDM | 18 | 48 | 0 | 0 | 18 | 48 |
| MELE | 4 | 12 | 0 | 0 | 4 | 12 |
| MEBA | 1 | 3 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 3 |
| MEAS | 8 | 18 | 0 | 0 | 8 | 18 |
| MEWH | 2 | 6 | 0 | 0 | 2 | 6 |
| **Total** | **320** | **620** | **2** | **3** | **322** | **623** |

The two nonzero promo rows are **Black Arrow x2** and **Fatty Bolger x1**. The previous 623-copy total remains correct, but the expansion-only subtotal is **620**, with three promotional copies kept separately. This corrects the earlier tendency to count every METW-coded promo as a base METW expansion copy.

The row-level summary is stored in [jose-san-hero-expansion-summary.csv](../data/jose-san-hero-expansion-summary.csv), with the promo detail in [jose-san-promo-quantities.csv](../data/jose-san-promo-quantities.csv). The BGG archive currently lists 69 files and includes Jose-san's Arda deck example XLS; no newer Arda XLS/XLSX or .dek file was found in the archive.

A separate CardGameGeek listing of “Gandalf Gene's Arda Deck” is a derivative transcription of the original Council of Elrond post. It lists 178 main entries and explicitly says two hazards are missing; it is not an independent full-pool source. The current MeCCG.com Decks page has no deck entries.

The Library also contains a 2022 “cards for Arda Leon” workbook with no expansion IDs and several names not yet matched to the original ICE catalogue. It has not been imported: its provenance and official-card-only scope are not sufficiently established. No Dream Cards or custom entries from it have been admitted.
