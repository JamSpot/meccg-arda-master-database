# Research watch — 3 November 2026

## Summary

A previously overlooked community document contains **two complete sample Arda Intro Game card pools**, including card quantities by deck section:

- **METW + METD, no rares:** 505 cards across all listed piles.
- **All rarities and sets:** 606 cards across all listed piles.

This is a meaningful additional published card-pool source. It is a separate 2024 introductory Arda variant, not a replacement for the canonical Gandalf Gene list. **No canonical Arda quantities have been changed.**

The source copy is a 14-page PDF titled *The Arda Intro Game*, by Jared Brayshaw, last updated May 2024. A public download URL was not identified in this pass; the PDF is present in the project research Library.

## 1. What the PDF contains

The introductory variant is designed for 2–5 players and is based on the Compiled Arda Ruleset — Hero, Arda Revised, and Gandalf Gene's Arda Deck. Its shared card structure differs from the basic multiplayer Arda arrangement:

- Play Deck
- Marshalling Points (MP) Deck, with each MP resource paired with a site
- Character Deck
- Minor Item Deck
- Ring Deck
- Haven Deck
- Avatar Deck

The site is paired with the relevant MP resource in a clear sleeve rather than being managed in a separate site deck. This is therefore an Arda-specific teaching variant, not a conventional constructed Hero-vs-Hazard solo deck.

The official database also contains similarly named **Minion-alignment** printings in The Lidless Eye. The sample pool's Hero entries must be mapped to the corresponding Hero card IDs, not to those Minion versions. This is an important identity check for the master inventory.

The PDF includes **Dreams of Lore**, which is the original ICE Hero resource card TW-210 from The Wizards. It is not a Dream Card. No custom Dream Cards are being admitted to the project inventory.
